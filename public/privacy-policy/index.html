Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Brick Instructions For You handles information when you visit brickinstructionsforyou.com or when the site's own publishing tools connect to services such as Pinterest.

Who we are

Brick Instructions For You is an independent LEGO building-instructions website run by Paul in the United Kingdom. The site provides LEGO set information, embedded instruction videos, chapter links and links to relevant third-party services.

Information collected when you use the website

The website does not currently provide user accounts, process payments or ask visitors to create a profile. Like most websites, technical information may be generated automatically when pages are requested. This can include an IP address, browser and device information, approximate location derived from an IP address, pages viewed, referral information and the date and time of a request.

This information is used to operate, secure and improve the website, understand which instruction pages are useful to visitors and diagnose technical problems.

Analytics, cookies and similar technologies

Brick Instructions For You uses Google Analytics 4 to understand website usage and performance. Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies and may receive information about your browser, device and interaction with the site. The website is hosted and delivered through Cloudflare, which may also process technical request and security information as part of hosting, performance and abuse prevention.

Browser settings can be used to block or delete cookies. Blocking some technologies may affect how embedded or third-party features work.

Embedded YouTube videos and external links

Instruction pages can contain embedded YouTube videos. When a YouTube player is loaded or used, Google/YouTube may receive information about the visit in accordance with their own privacy practices. The site also links to external services including LEGO, Pinterest, Facebook and Instagram. Those services operate under their own privacy policies and are responsible for information they collect when you visit them.

Pinterest publishing integration

Brick Instructions For You uses a private internal publishing tool to connect its own Pinterest Business account to the Pinterest API. The purpose of the integration is to create Pins for newly published LEGO instruction pages, place those Pins on relevant boards and link them back to the matching page on brickinstructionsforyou.com.

The integration is not offered as a login or publishing service to third parties. Pinterest OAuth credentials and access tokens are used only to authorise the site's own publishing workflow. Operational identifiers such as Pinterest board IDs and Pin IDs may be stored so the automation can publish to the correct board and avoid duplicate Pins. These credentials and identifiers are not sold or used to build advertising profiles of website visitors.

How information is used

Information processed in connection with the website is used only for legitimate operational purposes such as providing pages and videos, measuring site performance, maintaining security, preventing abuse, improving navigation and content, and operating the site's own social-media publishing tools.

Sharing and service providers

Brick Instructions For You does not sell personal information. Technical information may be processed by service providers needed to run the website and its integrations, including Cloudflare, Google/YouTube, Google Analytics and Pinterest. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect the website, its users or legal rights.

Data retention and security

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described above or as required by the relevant service provider. Reasonable technical measures are used to protect credentials and operational data. Pinterest API credentials are kept outside the public website code and are not intentionally exposed to visitors.

Your UK data-protection rights

Depending on the circumstances, UK data-protection law may give you rights relating to personal information about you, including rights to request access, correction or deletion, restrict or object to certain processing, and complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office. Some requests may be limited where information is not held directly by Brick Instructions For You or is controlled by a third-party service provider.

Children's privacy

The website is a general LEGO reference resource and does not knowingly ask children to create accounts or directly submit personal information through the site.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the website, analytics setup or third-party integrations change. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last updated.
